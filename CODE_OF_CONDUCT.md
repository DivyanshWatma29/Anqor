# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Anqor is committed to a respectful, technically focused community. Participants should communicate professionally, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and keep discussions focused on the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, and abusive or threatening conduct.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or close contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and constructive.

For sensitive concerns, use the private security/reporting channels described in `SECURITY.md` rather than posting confidential information publicly.
